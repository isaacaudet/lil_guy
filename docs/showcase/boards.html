Lil Guys on Boards

How a player gets their guy and first items, and a light way to show what they wear on every board and in every game, with animation where it counts. Everything below is drawn by the library in this page, and the timings are measured here.

From a name to a dressed guy

On a leaderboard

Every row shows the guy in what it wears, as a still. Only the top three and your own row move; any row moves while you point at it. Before the library has even loaded, each face square already shows its best copy's grade as a coloured bar, from one number in the row.

    What keeps it light

    • One canvas per face, drawn once. Today each face is an SVG of up to 256 rectangles. A 16×16 canvas is one element and draws in a fraction of a millisecond.
    • Cached by what it shows. The same name, look and wear always draws the same pixels, so the cache key is those three strings.
    • The library loads late. As now, it's fetched when the first face is nearly on screen. Rows show at once with the grade bar.
    • Few things move. The top three, you, and whatever you point at. One shared timer at 12 frames a second drives them all, and it stops when they scroll away or the tab is hidden.
    • Frames are made once. A move's frames are built the first time it plays and reused.
    • Small rows. The row carries the copies' short codes, not pictures.

    In a game

    The results screen gets the same guy in the same items. It cheers on a good day and sulks on a bad one; the game asks club.js for a move and gets frames back.

    Three things to decide first
    
    
    
      
    Saved looks break
    The club already lets players pick their guy, saved as seven numbers like 3.5.0.12.4.0.17. Those are positions in the old parts list. The new library has different parts in different places, so every saved look would turn into someone else.
    My pick: version the code. Old codes keep the name's guy and the player is asked to pick again; new codes start with 2:.
    
      
    Renaming changes the guy
    The guy comes from the board name. Change the nickname and the guy changes, and so would starter copies made from the name.
    My pick: starters are made once on the server and stored, so they never change. Whether the guy follows a rename is your call: the look column can pin him.
    
      
    Picking vs rare traits
    If anyone can pick laser eyes and a crown, a rare guy means nothing.
    My pick: the picker keeps the common parts. Rare shapes, eyes and toppers only come from your name or as items, so they stay worth something.
