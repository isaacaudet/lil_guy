Lil Guy Review

Everything in the library as of 0.5, each option on four different guys: a pastel one, a tall mint one, a dark night one and a yellow one, because yellow is where gold parts vanish. Green borders are what I'd keep and show off, red what's ugly, amber what I'd consider cutting.

Verdict

Nicest

  • The core faces: shiny and sparkle eyes with a cat or laugh mouth. That's the charm.
  • Soft shapes: round, mochi, gumdrop, onigiri, slime, ghost.
  • Rare shapes that work: jellyfish, octopus, toadstool, heart, saucer.
  • Toppers: cat and bunny ears, sprout, bow, mushroom, beanie, witch hat, propeller.
  • Skins: jar of gems, toy block, water bottle, racer, traffic cone, hamster ball, crystal.
  • Frames: checkered flag, patchwork, gingham, bricks, waves, spiral.
  • Finishes: Nebula, Prism and Gold, plus the gold rim at a year of tally.

Ugliest

  • Gold on yellow: star eyes, gold-leaf and star cheeks, star, moon and bolt marks, starry, star sticker, crown, unicorn horn, trophy. They vanish.
  • Shapes that misread: bat looks like a cat, crab's claws like antlers, cactus lost its arms. (Flame is redrawn.)
  • Laser eyes look like blush.
  • Lucky night colours go brown and olive.
  • Eye patch reads as hair. Headphones melt into the head.
  • Tiny hats that are just a lump: little block, tower, bottle cap, landed bottle.
  • The 2-pixel pins (leaf, bolt).

Consider removing

  • Peanut and bean: the same tall oval as pill and tower.
  • Glance and content eyes: near copies of shiny and happy.
  • Dot cheeks (a weak blush), gleam marking (barely there).
  • Stripes and zigzag markings, whiskers: too faint at 16 pixels. Redraw or cut.
  • Chick tuft: the tuft again. Eggshell: reads as a bald patch.
  • Two of the four lump hats, and the flat Lavender and Kitchen teal frames.

Consider adding

  • A buddy: a 4×4 pet beside him (a tiny golf ball, a chick, a slime). A new slot people would chase.
  • Held items: a golf club, a flag, a spatula. One per game.
  • Eyes: sleepy, dizzy spirals, button eyes, X eyes for a loss.
  • Toppers: a ponytail, curls, a tiara, a viking helmet, a flower crown.
  • Shapes that read instantly: a star, a dumpling, a cat loaf, a teardrop.
  • Patterned bodies as a rare marking: calico, galaxy, polka dots.
  • Seasonal items with a date stamp, and a third skin for the games with the fewest.
