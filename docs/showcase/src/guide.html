Lil Guy Guide

The whole system on one page: who your guy is, what he wears, what makes a copy rare, and how you get it.

1

Your guy comes from your name

Eight parts picked by the name. He never changes and looks the same in every game. About 1 name in 400 is Lucky.

2

Items: wear your games

Every game has 3 skins (he becomes the game's object), 3 hats (one free) and 2 frames. 72 in all.

3

A copy is one of a kind

When you own an item, your copy rolls its own details.

4

5

It grows as you play

Nothing here can be bought. A copy's tally counts the days you finish wearing it; pins and emotes are earned.

6

How you get things

FreeA starter hat for each game, the day you first play it.
Daily capsuleFinish the gauntlet, open one capsule free. Same machine, same odds.
More capsulesThe same machine, for tickets.
ShopA rotating shelf of Plain items, for tickets.
Swap upFive of one grade for one of the next.

Tickets come from playing. Rare finishes only come from capsules and swap-ups, never the shelf, so they stay rare.

7

Shown everywhere

On boards as a still with a grade bar; the top three and you move. In games he cheers, sulks, dances and sleeps.
