Proposed: ★ Relics, the tier above Grail

The knife of the machines: about 1 in 1,000 capsules, from any machine, rarer than any Grail, with its own finishes and something you see every time the guy moves. Two ways to draw it.

Option A · held

A relic in his hand

A ★ item he holds, tip up into the empty top corner. Reads best as a lollipop, cleaver or wand; a thin blade is a squeeze at 16 pixels.

Option B · aura

An aura round him

A ★ effect in the empty pixels round him, moving all the time, so it shows on a board row as well as on a card.
