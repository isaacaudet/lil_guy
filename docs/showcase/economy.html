Lil Guy Economy

600 simulated players over 90 days, rolling with the library's real drop odds (Gold is 1 in 400), to test stamps and capsule prices before real players do. A stamp for each club game finished, two gold for clearing the gauntlet and ten to start; a mystery capsule is 5, a picked machine 8. The daily drop is a free capsule for finishing the day's gauntlet. Move the numbers; everything reruns.
