Float

Prints

★ Auras

One capsule in a thousand, from any machine. An aura goes round your guy and moves with him. Its float thins it, its print sets its timing, and it has its own finishes.

Effects

A rare layer on top of the finish. About one item in fifteen has one, and about one aura in three, so an aura's effect is its version. The last three are the rarest.

What makes one copy special

Two copies of the same item in the same finish are still different. Collectors look for these.

Trade up

Five copies of one grade, none worn, go into the machine. Three times in four the next grade comes out, one time in five a dud at the same grade, one in twenty two grades up. Its float is the five's average place in their finishes, so clean inputs make a clean result, and it comes from one of their machines, each as likely as its share of the five. Trades stop at Grail: auras only come out of capsules.
